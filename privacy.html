Privacy policy

Baxter (registered with Google as “friday”). Last updated 30 October 2026.

Baxter is a personal app used only by the person who built it. This page says what happens to data it gets from Google.

What it accesses

With permission, Baxter reads and searches my Gmail and creates and sends drafts. It asks for gmail.readonly and gmail.compose only. It cannot delete or change existing mail.

How it is used

  • Email is read only to answer what I ask, like “any new email?” or “reply to this”.
  • Email I ask about is sent to Google's Gemini API to produce the answer. Google's Gemini API terms apply to that.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with anyone else, or used for advertising.

Where it is kept

The Google sign-in token and Baxter's conversation history are stored on my own computers only. Nothing is uploaded to a Baxter server; there isn't one.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the credentials folder on the computer removes the saved sign-in.
